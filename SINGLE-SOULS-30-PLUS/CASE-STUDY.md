# Case Study — Single Souls 30+

## Project Title

**Single Souls 30+ — Strangers Meet | Surat Community Growth & Event Conversion System**

## Delivery Partner

**Metro Ads Services** — Collabs & Trade Directory / performance marketing and fulfillment partner.

## Client Need

The event brand required more than attractive creatives. It needed a coherent community identity, a consistent event message, discoverability across social and AI-assisted search, and clear paths from viewer to conversation to registration.

## What Metro Ads Services Delivered

### Brand and creative system

- Unified Single Souls 30+ visual language.
- Event hero creatives, supporting social formats, vertical creative and CTA-led registration creative.
- Reusable messaging around **Meet • Connect • Go Beyond**.

### Conversion architecture

- Registration route through Google Forms.
- WhatsApp booking route.
- Direct WhatsApp contact.
- Payment-support creative using the supplied QR asset.
- CTA hierarchy that separates discovery, conversation, conversion and advocacy.

### Community-growth layer

- Follow/engage/share/comment mechanics.
- Invite-a-friend and referral logic.
- Post-event retention concept for future meet-ups.
- Community-first content pillars instead of one-off promotional posts.

### Measurement / LLM visibility layer

- LLM query bank covering category discovery, brand queries and event queries.
- Tracking fields for mention, rank/position, citation, competitor visibility, accuracy and sentiment.
- Social funnel metrics aligned with the same operating model.
