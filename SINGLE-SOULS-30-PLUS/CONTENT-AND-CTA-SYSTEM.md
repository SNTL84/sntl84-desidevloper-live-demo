# Content + CTA System

## Content Pillars

### 1. Event Discovery
Hook examples:

- **30+ & single? This one's for you.**
- **Strangers today. Friends tomorrow?**
- **New people. Real conversations. A more happening you.**

CTA: **See event details**

### 2. Experience
Focus on dinner, games, mingling, conversation and a respectful environment.

CTA: **Book your spot**

### 3. Social Proof
Use genuine attendee photos, reactions, testimonials and post-event moments after consent.

CTA: **Follow for the next meet**

### 4. Objection Handling
Answer common questions about age, format, venue sharing, price, registration, payment and what happens after registration.

CTA: **Ask on WhatsApp**

### 5. Community Retention
Show that the brand is bigger than one event: people, stories, conversations and future meet-ups.

CTA: **Join the community**

## CTA Hierarchy

**Primary:** Register Now / Book Your Spot  
**Secondary:** WhatsApp Us  
**Tertiary:** Follow @soulsingle30  
**Advocacy:** Invite a friend / Share this post

## Live Button Design Principle

Every major page/post should provide at least one native clickable action where the platform supports it. Where native buttons are unavailable, use a short link in the caption/profile and a repeated visual CTA.

Do not overload a creative with five equal-weight calls to action. Pick the one action that matches the content's stage in the funnel.

## Example Conversion Path

**Reel:** "30+ & single?" → **Comment / Follow**  
↓  
**Profile:** event proof + date + city + price → **WhatsApp / Register**  
↓  
**WhatsApp:** booking help + payment instructions → **Confirmation**  
↓  
**Event:** photos + conversations → **Share / Refer**  
↓  
**Post-event:** recap + next meet → **Retention**
