# Metro Ads Services — Suggested Repeatable Scope

## Tier 1 — Event Launch Foundation

Brand cleanup, event messaging, core creatives, profile/cover updates, link hygiene and registration CTA setup.

## Tier 2 — Conversion Campaign

Everything in Tier 1 plus paid-campaign creative variants, audience testing plan, WhatsApp handoff, registration funnel and weekly optimization/reporting.

## Tier 3 — Community Growth Retainer

Everything in Tier 2 plus recurring content calendar, engagement mechanics, event recaps, referral/retention campaigns, LLM/GEO visibility tracking and monthly executive report.

## Standard Deliverables

- Brand asset pack
- Event creative pack
- Copy bank
- CTA/link inventory
- Registration / WhatsApp conversion map
- Campaign QA checklist
- LLM visibility tracker
- Growth funnel tracker
- Case-study update

## Commercial Positioning

Use this scope to show event managers exactly what they are buying. Avoid selling "posts" as the unit of value. Sell a measurable **event growth and conversion system**.

## Reuse Model

`MASTER TEMPLATE → NEW CITY → NEW EVENT DATE → NEW CREATIVE PACK → NEW CTA LINKS → SAME TRACKING SYSTEM → VERIFIED CASE STUDY`
