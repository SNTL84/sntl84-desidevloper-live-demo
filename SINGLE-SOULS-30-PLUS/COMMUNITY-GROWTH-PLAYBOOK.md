# Community Growth Playbook

## Operating Principle

The goal is not to maximize vanity reach. The goal is to turn attention into a **growing owned community**.

### Growth loop

**Discover → Engage → Follow → Message → Register → Attend → Share → Refer → Return**

## Weekly Content Cadence

### Monday — Discovery
Short hook/reel around the 30+ single-life pain point or desire for better social circles.

### Tuesday — Conversation
Question post: "What makes a great evening with new people after 30?"

### Wednesday — Experience
Dinner/games/mingling visual showing the event format.

### Thursday — Trust
FAQ, safety/respect expectations, what registration includes, venue-sharing process.

### Friday — CTA
High-intent registration reminder with direct link/WhatsApp button.

### Weekend — Community
Behind-the-scenes, event preparation or genuine post-event stories.

## Engagement Mechanics

Use prompts that encourage genuine interaction:

- "Tag a friend who should come."
- "What would you rather join: games or conversation rounds?"
- "Would you come alone or with a friend?"
- "What is one thing that makes social events feel comfortable after 30?"

Do not fabricate comments, attendees, testimonials or social proof.

## Conversion Hygiene

Before launch, verify:

1. Registration form opens.
2. WhatsApp booking link opens.
3. Contact number is correct.
4. Payment instructions are correct.
5. Event date/time/price match every asset.
6. Venue disclosure language is consistent.

## Post-Event Retention

Within 24–48 hours, publish an honest recap using approved photos/videos, thank attendees, invite stories, and route people toward the next event.

The most valuable metric after an event is not only attendance; it is whether attendees become **repeat community participants and referrers**.
