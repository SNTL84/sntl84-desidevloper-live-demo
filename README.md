**Reach · Impressions · Engagement Rate · Profile Visits · Follows · Shares · Saves · WhatsApp Starts · Form Starts · Registrations · Paid Confirmations · Attendance · Referrals · Repeat Enquiries**

For LLM/GEO visibility, keep a repeatable prompt bank and record:

**Brand Mention · Position · Citation/Source · Accuracy · Sentiment · Competitors Mentioned · Date Checked · Visibility Score**

Performance numbers must be reported only from verified analytics, registration records, payment records, attendance logs or other auditable sources. **No invented reach, conversions, ROI, testimonials or AI visibility.**
